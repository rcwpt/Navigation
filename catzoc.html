Understanding CATZOC

CATZOC highlights the reliability of bathymetric data within an ENC. It affects your Under Keel Clearance (UKC) calculations.

  • A1 & A2: High accuracy. Recent, full-bottom search surveys.
  • B & C: Moderate. Older surveys, possible uncharted hazards.
  • D & U: Poor or Unassessed. Large errors expected; navigate with extreme caution.
