GMDSS Maintenance Directives

Mandatory routine maintenance and operational verification procedures compliant with SOLAS Chapter IV regulations.

Daily

  • Verify VHF DSC internal connections & display via built-in test.
  • Execute MF/HF DSC internal diagnostic (no external transmission).
  • Confirm INMARSAT-C login status to appropriate Ocean Region.
  • Check reserve source of energy (batteries) status & printer paper.

Weekly

  • Transmit DSC test call to Coast Station (MF/HF).
  • Verify internal comms between duplicate VHF sets.
  • Operate equipment from reserve source of energy (if applicable).
  • Log vessel-to-vessel test if coast station test fails.

Monthly

  • INMARSAT-C Performance Verification (PV).
  • EPIRB self-test (check physical condition & HRU expiry).
  • SARTs visual check & live radar test (avoid port interference).
  • Detailed inspection of emergency batteries & connections.

Radio Log

  • Record exact vessel position (Lat/Long) daily.
  • Log all distress, urgency, and safety communications.
  • Record details of all maintenance and testing (Pass/Fail).
  • Master must review and sign the logbook daily.
